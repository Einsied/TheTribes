\subsection{Society}\label{ch:Tribes:Japanese:Society}

The \gls{Japanese} prepare their society to accept members from all tribes.
To ensure that the dragon emperors domain functions smoothly,
all subjects are trained according to their interests,
before they are assigned a permanent role.
This role is determined by the great examination,
before it they choice of occupation is way less restricted.

\subsubsection{Social classes}\label{ch:Tribes:Japanese:Society:Classes}

The subjects of the \gls{Japanese} are split into four main and two special classes.
The four main classes are named after the dragon emperors themselves,
with a focus on their primary duty

\begin{itemize}
	\item Black scales:
		Dealing with all animate things, like farming, forestry or hunting
	\item White scales:
		Dealing with all inanimate things, like mining, smiting, weaving
	\item Black flames:
		Tasked with protecting the domain of the dragon emperors,
		these are the soldiers.
	\item White flames:
		Tasked with administration and research.
\end{itemize}

The other two classes are the unexamined,
which are in the process of joining one of the other classes
and the servants.
The servants are the priests of the dragon emperors
recruited from all other classes and tasked with overseeing the examinations,
maintaining the temples and casting the dragons magic.
Every temple of the dragon emperors has three priests to fullfil this roles.

\subsubsection{Professions}\label{ch:Tribes:Japanese:Professions}

The \hyperref[ch:Tribes:Japanese]{imperial} \hyperref[ch:Tribes:Japanese:Economy]{economy}
has of professions to fill. 
While most can be occupied without special education,
educated subjects are usually more productive.

Some professions require \glspl{Tool} to do their work or special workplaces.
Some professions are more mentally taxing others are physically exhausting,
leading to an increase in the
\hyperref[ch:World:ch:World:Inhabitants:Sapients:Humans]{needs}
for \gls{Food} and \gls{Sleep}.

	\midrule
	Hunter     & Unexamined, Black scales & \Gls{Weapon} & Improves  & Higher     & Medium      \\
	\bottomrule
\end{longtable}
